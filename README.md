# 🏥 Healthcare Provider Performance & Clinical Analytics Dashboard

# 1. Recommended Structure & Project Headline
Headline
Executive Healthcare Provider Analytics: Patient Outcomes, Operational Throughput & Revenue Cycle Management Dashboard

# 2. Short Description & Purpose
Executive Summary
The Healthcare Provider Analytics Dashboard is an enterprise-grade reporting solution engineered in Power BI. It consolidates disparate clinical, operational, and billing records into an interactive, multi-tier management cockpit.
Designed for hospital administrators, department heads, and clinical directors, this report monitors:
●	Patient Journey & Flow: Inpatient admissions, length of stay (ALOS), and bed turnover rates.
●	Clinical Quality & Safety: 30-day readmission rates, mortality indices, and post-discharge complications.
●	Provider & Staff Utilization: Doctor/nurse allocation, departmental workload, and consultation throughput.
●	Revenue Cycle Management (RCM): Average claim turnaround times, denial ratios, and insurance vs. self-pay billing distribution.
Key Business & Clinical Objectives
1.	Reduce Wait Times & Length of Stay: Identify bottlenecks across triage, inpatient wards, and emergency departments.
2.	Improve Patient Retention & Outcomes: Track readmission patterns across diagnoses, age brackets, and care teams.
3.	Optimize Facility Resource Allocation: Monitor bed utilization thresholds to prevent overcrowding.
4.	Ensure Regulatory & Billing Compliance: Detect claim processing bottlenecks to maintain healthy cash flow.

# 3. Tech Stack
List the key technologies used to build the dashboard.

Example: The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
• 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.
• 📂 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.
• 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic.
• 📝 Data Modeling – Relationships established among tables (resorts, snow, and data_dictionary) to enable cross-filtering and aggregation.
• 📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard previews.

# 4. Features & Key Highlights
Multi-Page Executive Navigation
●	📊 Page 1: Executive KPI Scorecard
○	High-level KPIs: Total Admissions, Bed Occupancy Rate (%), Average Length of Stay (ALOS), Readmission Rate (%), and Net Patient Margin.
○	Variance indicators comparing current metrics against internal benchmarks and prior-year performance.
●	🛏️ Page 2: Operational Flow & Capacity Management
○	Departmental occupancy heatmaps identifying ward congestion and bottleneck triage areas.
○	Admission source decomposition (Emergency vs. Elective vs. Referral).
●	🩺 Page 3: Clinical Outcomes & Care Quality
○	Diagnostic breakdown with drill-down into specific ICD codes and treatment durations.
○	Readmission analysis categorized by patient demographic bands and provider teams.
●	💳 Page 4: Revenue Cycle & Departmental Performance
○	Reimbursement rates and claim settlement times across primary insurance payers.
○	Provider throughput matrix highlighting encounter volume and resource utilization.
Technical Highlights
●	Responsive Cross-Filtering: Selecting a specific medical department or age band dynamically filters provider performance and clinical outcomes across all cards and visuals.
●	Time Intelligence Analysis: Built-in DAX patterns calculating Month-over-Month (MoM), Year-over-Year (YoY), and rolling 30-day patient metrics.
●	Clean Clinical UI: Card visual hierarchies, intuitive color coding for risk alerts (e.g., occupancy above 85%), and minimal cognitive load for clinical staff.
